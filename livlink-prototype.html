Automation rules

Seeded example

Three supported IF–THEN rules — not a general automation language.

IF Leaving Home is triggered THEN arm security, cool to eco temperature (28°C), turn off unused lights
IF indoor temperature > 28°C THEN turn on AC at last preferred setting
IF ambient sunlight reads low (dusk / overcast) THEN gradually raise indoor lighting to a comfortable level

Seeded example: at 6:52 PM the simulated light sensor read 180 lux (dusk); the living room lights were raised from 40% to 70% automatically.
